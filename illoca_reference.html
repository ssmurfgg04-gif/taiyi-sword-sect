To those who shape the world,

Design was never meant to happen through buttons, commands, and rigid workflows.

It begins more naturally than that, through sketches, markups, references, conversation, and iteration. Through the back-and-forth between instinct and judgment that gives architecture its life.

But for too long, that flow has been interrupted, asking you to translate, redraw, rebuild, and re-explain instead of simply design.

We built Tracing Paper for the way this work actually happens: a place where intent can be expressed more directly, and where the production burden fades into the background.

So you can explore more freely, refine more quickly, and stay closer to the ideas that matter most.

This is for you.

Tracing Paper is an AI-native design engine built for the controllability required by professional practice. It bridges the gap between creative exploration and technical production:

  • Beyond Image-Based AI: Unlike generic tools that produce static pixels, Tracing Paper understands architectural intent. It generates structured, parametric 2D and 3D schematics that are design-ready. Rather than working through prompts alone, you iterate through sketches and markups, shaping outputs into functional, professional-grade design data.
  • Enhancing Conventional Workflows: While conventional CAD and BIM software often require manual, high-effort translation of ideas, Tracing Paper accepts natural inputs—sketches, redlines, and texts—to generate design documentation in real time. It is designed to be integrated into existing ecosystems like Autodesk Revit and Rhino, meeting architects in their current workflow to streamline the transition from vision to execution.

Tracing Paper is an intelligent canvas that replaces the traditional hundred-button toolset with a workflow that mirrors how design studios actually work: sketching, marking up, and pinning ideas.

It supports two modes: Ideate, where a rough sketch or verbal prompt becomes an immediate entry point for architectural drawings; and Iterate, where you refine concepts through redlines and markups while the system updates geometry and documentation in real-time. It is designed to be your "Thinking Interface," allowing you to export high-fidelity work directly into Autodesk Revit once your design is set.

Tracing Paper is built on a "Human-in-the-Loop" philosophy. You don’t need to be a "prompt engineer"—if you know how to redline a drawing or describe a change to a colleague, you know how to use Tracing Paper. While the Agent provides high-fidelity starting points for massing and floor plans based on real-world constraints, your professional judgment remains unmediated. You have full control to navigate tradeoffs between aesthetics, function, and client needs, ensuring the output is always a precision reflection of your intent.

You own your designs 100%. All outputs generated or refined within the platform are your intellectual property. We do not use your proprietary sketches, prompts, or project models to train our models. For our Enterprise partners, we offer additional security protocols, custom-trained models, and zero-data retention to ensure your firm’s proprietary design logic and "secret sauce" remain strictly confidential.

We believe in charging for professional output, not curiosity. You aren’t charged for navigation, sketching, or using the interface; credits are only consumed when the Agent performs high-value tasks including:

  • Image Generation: 10 credits per image
  • Agent Chats: 10 credits per turn
  • Agent Edits: 25 credits per turn
  • Floor Plan Generation: typically 15-150 credits, based on floor plan size and complexity
  • 3D Massing Generation: typically 10-120 credits, based on building size and complexity
  • Revit Exports: typically 20-240 credits, based on building size and complexity

To get started, every new user receives 1,000 free starter credits to experience the power of Tracing Paper firsthand.
